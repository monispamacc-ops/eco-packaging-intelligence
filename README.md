# 📦 Sustainable Packaging Market Intelligence & Feasibility Portal

An interactive B2B decision-support platform designed to evaluate market demand, techno-economic cost parity, and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) regulatory savings for bio-based paper packaging adoption across Indian industrial hubs.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Motive
With India enforcing strict Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) penalties on single-use plastics, enterprise clients in FMCG, e-commerce, and pharmaceuticals face regulatory and commercial challenges. This portal bridges material science and commercial strategy, enabling business development teams to provide prospective enterprise buyers with data-backed cost, compliance, and performance metrics.

---

## Key Features
* **Regional Demand Hotspot Mapping:** Analyzes addressable market volume (Metric Tons) and year-over-year (YoY) growth rates across key manufacturing clusters (NCR, Mumbai/Pune, Bengaluru, etc.).
* **Techno-Economic Cost Simulator:** Real-time financial modeling comparing multi-layer plastic packaging against bio-coated paper, factoring in base unit costs and EPR regulatory tax penalties.
* **Carbon Footprint Tracking:** Calculates greenhouse gas emission reductions ($\text{CO}_2\text{e}$) achieved by replacing traditional plastics.
